Summary

This team consulting project supported South Dakota Manufacturing and Technology Solutions by organizing and interpreting manufacturing supply-chain information. The broader team deliverables included supply-chain mapping, dashboard-style tools, data validation, and a written gap analysis intended to help economic-development stakeholders better understand manufacturing clusters and supply-chain opportunities across South Dakota.

Problem / Research Question

How can manufacturing and supplier data be organized, mapped, and interpreted to help identify regional supply-chain gaps and economic-development opportunities in South Dakota?

Data and Methods

The project involved company-level manufacturing and supplier information, validation work, mapping tools, and dashboard-style analysis. The team worked to organize business information into a more usable format for decision-makers.

Methods included:

Company data organization
Business validation
Supply-chain mapping
Dashboard-style data presentation
Regional gap analysis
Economic-development interpretation
Client-facing report writing
My Role

This was a team project, and the technical mapping/dashboard work was distributed across the group. My primary contributions were in the later stages of the project, especially interpretation, written analysis, presentation preparation, and translating the dashboard and map outputs into practical economic-development recommendations.

I would not describe this as a solo technical build. Instead, I view it as a consulting project where I contributed to the communication and interpretation of technical deliverables produced by the team.

Key Findings

The project helped organize manufacturing and supply-chain information in a form that could be used better to understand regional clusters, gaps, and opportunities. The final deliverables were intended to help stakeholders think more clearly about where supplier relationships, industry concentrations, and economic-development opportunities may exist.

Why This Project Matters

This project demonstrates experience working on a real client-facing analytics problem involving messy business data, mapping, supply-chain interpretation, and economic-development strategy. It also shows the importance of translating technical outputs into recommendations that nontechnical stakeholders can use.
