# About

I am an economics and business analytics graduate from the University of South Dakota with applied experience in econometrics, forecasting, optimization, finance, and client-facing consulting.

My undergraduate work combined economic theory, technical analytics, and professional communication. I completed projects involving public finance, macro-financial research, regional economic development, portfolio optimization, time-series forecasting, survey research, market concentration analysis, and project finance.

This portfolio highlights selected academic, technical, and consulting work completed during my undergraduate studies. The goal is to show not only the tools I used, but also how I translated analysis into clear recommendations for business, policy, and stakeholder audiences.
